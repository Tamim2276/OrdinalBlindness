The authors thank both reviewers for their constructive comments. Each comment
is answered below; section, table and figure numbers refer to the revised manuscript.

\textbf{Reviewer \#2:} \textit{``\ldots stronger experimental validation through
ablation studies, additional recent baselines, independent validation data,
repeated experiments, and detailed class-wise performance analysis\ldots''}

{\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}L{3.1cm}L{11.0cm}L{2.4cm}@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Request} & \textbf{Response} & \textbf{Where} \\
\midrule
Ablation studies &
Each component was ablated under $\alpha=0.1$ and the real-site partition:
OrdinalLoss with sample-count weights, cross-entropy with QWK weights,
cross-entropy with uniform weights, and full OrdinalFed.
\TBD{one-sentence finding from the ablation table} &
Sec.~IV-D, Table~\TBD{n} \\
Additional baselines &
A quality-aware aggregation baseline (AccWt), representative of server-side
validation scoring [9], was added. It uses OrdinalFed's scoring and schedule but
weights clients by accuracy instead of QWK, isolating the value of the ordinal
signal. \TBD{one-sentence comparison} &
Sec.~IV-C, Table~I \\
Independent validation data &
All headline results are now reported on the DDR test split excluding the 50
server-set images (3,709 images), which is never used for training,
aggregation or model selection; the reported round is chosen by validation QWK. &
Sec.~IV-D, Table~I \\
Repeated experiments &
Every configuration was repeated with \TBD{3} random seeds and is reported as
mean $\pm$ standard deviation. &
Tables~I--\TBD{n} \\
Class-wise analysis &
Per-grade recall and F1 on the test split are reported for FedAvg, FedProx and
OrdinalFed. \TBD{one sentence on Grades 1 and 3} &
Sec.~V-\TBD{x}, Table~\TBD{n} \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
}

\textit{Further corrections:} Eqs.~(4)--(6) now state the exact aggregation used in
the experiments; the mechanistic analysis (Sec.~V-C) was rewritten from the logged
per-round server scores and aggregation weights; the server-side overhead is reported
as measured (\TBD{x}\,s per round); the dataset statistics in Sec.~IV-A were corrected.
