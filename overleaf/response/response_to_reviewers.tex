We thank both reviewers for their careful reading and helpful comments. Each
point is answered below. Section, table and figure numbers refer to the revised
manuscript.

\textbf{Reviewer \#2:} \textit{``\ldots stronger experimental validation through
ablation studies, additional recent baselines, independent validation data,
repeated experiments, and detailed class-wise performance analysis\ldots''}

{\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}L{3.1cm}L{11.0cm}L{2.4cm}@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Request} & \textbf{What we changed} & \textbf{Where} \\
\midrule
Ablation studies &
We removed one component at a time, under $\alpha=0.1$ and the real-site
partition: OrdinalLoss with image-count weights, cross-entropy with QWK weights,
cross-entropy with equal weights, and the full method.
\TBD{one-sentence finding from the ablation table} &
Sec.~IV-D, Table~\TBD{n} \\
Additional baselines &
We added AccWt, a quality-weighted baseline based on server-side validation
scoring [9]. It is identical to our aggregation except that clients are scored by
accuracy instead of QWK, so it shows whether the ordinal score itself matters.
\TBD{one-sentence comparison} &
Sec.~IV-C, Table~I \\
Independent validation data &
All main results now come from the DDR test split (3,709 images, excluding the
50 server-set images). These images are never used for training, aggregation or
choosing the reported round, which is selected on the validation split. All
models score lower on the test split; this is now discussed in the Limitations. &
Sec.~IV-D, Table~I \\
Repeated experiments &
Every configuration was run with \TBD{3} random seeds; results are reported as
mean $\pm$ standard deviation. &
Tables~I--\TBD{n} \\
Class-wise analysis &
We now report per-grade recall and F1 on the test split for FedAvg, FedProx and
OrdinalFed. \TBD{one sentence on Grades 1 and 3} &
Sec.~V-\TBD{x}, Table~\TBD{n} \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
}

\textit{Other corrections:} Eqs.~(4)--(6) now describe exactly the aggregation used
in the experiments. The analysis of why OrdinalFed is stable (Sec.~V-C) was
rewritten from the per-round server scores and aggregation weights logged during
training. The server-side overhead is now the measured value (\TBD{x}\,s per round),
and the dataset statistics in Sec.~IV-A were corrected.
