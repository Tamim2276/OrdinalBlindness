\section{Conclusion}

OrdinalFed, a federated framework for multi-grade Diabetic Retinopathy screening
that combines a distance-aware OrdinalLoss with QWK-weighted aggregation, has been
evaluated on the DDR benchmark with three seeds and a held-out test split. Under
extreme label skew, its server-side weighting prevents most of FedAvg's collapse
(worst-round test QWK 0.514 versus 0.378) at no communication cost. The ablations
show that this benefit comes from giving small hospitals more weight: equal client
weights perform better, a 50-image server set is too small for the QWK scores to
rank clients, and the OrdinalLoss does not help in the federated setting. FedProx
remains the most stable method, and combining it with OrdinalFed comes close to,
but does not exceed, FedProx alone. On realistic multi-site and IID partitions, all
methods perform alike. Future work will test larger server sets, higher image
resolution and cross-dataset validation on APTOS~2019, and will explore formal
differential privacy guarantees.
